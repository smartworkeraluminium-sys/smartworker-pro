Smart Worker Pro (smartworker pro Aluminum)
​Smart Worker Pro is a fast, Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA) built for Aluminium, uPVC & Glass Fabrication Workshops across India.
​🔗 Live Web App: https://smartworkeraluminium-sys.github.io/smartworker-pro/
​Core Fabrication Modules
​Aluminium Systems
​Euro Series & Sliding Windows: 2-Track, 3-Track, 4-Track, Interlock & Jali cutting formulas.
​Euro Casement (Euro 40 / 42 / 45 / 50): Interactive Tree-Split CAD Designer with Fix, Open, French, Top-Hung, Jali & ACP bays.
​Partition Master & Commercial Doors: Office Glass + ACP partitions, 4-inch bottom kickplate doors, and ceiling estimator.
​Bar & Glass Optimizers: 12 ft / 16 ft Aluminium Bar Cutting Optimizer and 4×8 Glass / ACP Sheet Cutting Optimizer.
​uPVC Pro Systems
​uPVC Casement & French Windows: Welding allowance (+6 mm), GI Steel reinforcement, 45° Glazing Bead & Glass calculation.
​uPVC Sliding (2T / 2.5T / 3T) & uPVC Doors: Universal Frame F, Sash S, Bead Height (BH) & With/Without Bottom Frame formulas.
​uPVC Profile Cutting (5.95 m / 19.5 ft): Bar optimization + 6.0 m GI Steel, EPDM Gasket, Wool Pile & Hardware estimator.
​Colours & Glass Grade: 12 Indian uPVC profile finishes and explicit NORMAL / ANNEALED vs TOUGHENED / DGU glass stamping.
​Workshop Business & Billing
​Site Measurement Khata: Room-wise tracking, saved records, and customer due book.
​Pro Quotation & Cutting PDFs: Vector CAD Inside View schematics, CGST/SGST or IGST, UPI QR, Bank Details, Glass QR labels & Hardware PDFs.
​3-Language Interface: English, বাংলা (Bengali) & हिन्दी (Hindi), with 100% English workshop PDF exports.
​Offline-First PWA: Works directly on Android phones even on construction sites without internet.
